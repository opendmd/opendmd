# Set up a card

Fill a microSD card with the DMDoS GIF pack, straight from this page: the
GIFs and playlists exactly as the pack has them, each GIF ready to play
from the first boot. The card keeps working with DMDoS too.

> **Works in:** Chrome or Edge on a computer: Windows, macOS, Linux or
> ChromeOS. Other browsers, phones and tablets can't write to a card from
> a web page: copy the pack onto the card yourself as
> [The SD card](card.md) describes, and the player gets the GIFs ready by
> itself over a night or two.

## Get the GIF pack

The GIF collection is made by the DMDoS community and offered on
[dmdos.net](https://dmdos.net/), in its Software section: download
**DMDos GUI v1 + GIFs** from there. It's a zip of about 1 GB; there's no
need to unzip it.

Nothing is uploaded: this page reads the zip on your computer and writes
straight to the card.

## Write the card

Put the card in your computer (a USB card reader works). Drop the zip
below, click **Write to the card…** and pick the card itself, the drive
that holds it, not a folder on it. Your browser asks whether this site may
edit files there: allow it.

## Good to know

- **The card needs FAT32,** like every card the player reads. Most cards
  up to 32 GB come that way; bigger ones are usually exFAT, and the player
  can't read those. See [Using a new card](card.md#using-a-new-card) for
  formatting one.
- **It takes a while:** usually 10 to 25 minutes for the whole pack,
  depending on the card and the reader, sometimes more. The time left
  shows as it goes.
- **Stop whenever you like.** Nothing is half-written: pick the card again
  and the page carries on where it stopped, skipping what's already there.
  Running it on a card that's already finished writes nothing.
- **A card that already has the pack** (copied by hand) works too: the
  page leaves its GIFs as they are and only gets them ready to play.
- **What goes on the card:** the `gifs` folder, the playlists
  (`filelist.txt`, `filelist.hall` and `filelist.xmas`) and the DMDoS boot
  logo. The DMDoS Windows program in the zip isn't needed on the card.
  Nothing already on the card is deleted.
- **Your own GIFs** go on the card later from the web app's
  [Library](web-app.md#library), or make some with
  [Create Art](create-art.md).

Something not working? See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

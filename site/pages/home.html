For the DMDoS display

Your DMDoS display, with a web app

OpenDMD is free, open-source software for it. It plays the GIFs already on your SD card and adds a web app for your phone or computer. Choose what plays, build playlists, make your own art, and add Formulas: small animations you can change while they run.

What you can do

You do it all in the web app, at opendmd.local, from any phone, tablet or computer on your WiFi.

  • Choose what playsSearch your GIFs, preview each with the panel's look, and send one to the panel.
  • PlaylistsBuild them by game system, category or title words, reorder them, and switch live.
  • Create ArtTurn game art, an old GeoCities GIF or your own picture into a GIF made for the panel.
  • FormulasTen come with OpenDMD, and the gallery has many more to add.
  • Looks over your GIFsLay scanlines, an old TV, a vignette or glitter over any GIF.
  • Colour and night modeFull colour or a single amber, red, green, blue or white, a soft glow, and a schedule that dims the panel at night.

What you need

  • A DMDoS displayTwo LED panels, an ESP32 board and a microSD card. dmdos.net has the parts list and a build guide with photos.
  • A computer, onceChrome, Edge, Opera or Firefox 151+ and a USB cable, to install OpenDMD.
  • Your WiFiThe display joins your 2.4 GHz WiFi, and you use it from any browser on the same network.

More about what you need

OpenDMD and DMDoS

DMDoS is a community project from Spain. The display, its board by MORTACA, the build guide and the GIF pack all come from dmdos.net. OpenDMD only replaces the software on the display's ESP32 board, and the original DMDoS software can be put back from the Software section of dmdos.net.

OpenDMD is an independent project, not made by or affiliated with DMDoS or MORTACA. To build it yourself or see how it works, read For developers.

For the DMDoS LED display

Your DMDoS display, with a web app

OpenDMD is free, open-source software for the DMDoS LED display. It plays the GIFs already on your SD card, and adds a web app on your phone or computer: choose what plays, build playlists, make your own art, and add Formulas, little animations you can change as they run.

What you can do

Everything happens in the web app, at opendmd.local on any phone, tablet or computer on your WiFi. There's nothing to install on them.

  • Choose what playsSearch your GIFs, preview each with the panel's look, and send one to the panel.
  • PlaylistsBuild them by game system, category or title words, reorder them, and switch live.
  • Create ArtTurn game art, an old GeoCities GIF or your own picture into a GIF made for the panel.
  • FormulasTen come with the panel, and the gallery has many more to add.
  • Clock and textA clock and scrolling text, drawn live on the panel and usable in any playlist.
  • Looks and night modeFull colour or a single amber, red, green, blue or white, a soft glow, and a schedule that dims the panel at night.

What you need

  • A DMDoS displayTwo LED panels, an ESP32 board and a microSD card. dmdos.net has the parts list and a build guide with photos.
  • A computer, onceChrome, Edge, Opera or Firefox on a computer, and a USB cable, to install OpenDMD.
  • Your WiFiThe display joins your 2.4 GHz WiFi, and you use it from any browser on the same network.

More about what you need

OpenDMD and DMDoS

DMDoS is a community project from Spain: the display, its board (by MORTACA), the build guide and the GIF pack all come from dmdos.net. OpenDMD only replaces the software on the display's ESP32 board. A card set up for DMDoS plays on OpenDMD as it is, and the original firmware can be put back at any time from the Software section of dmdos.net.

OpenDMD is an independent project, not made by or affiliated with DMDoS or MORTACA. Building it yourself, or curious how it works? See For developers.

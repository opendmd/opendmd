For the DMDoS display

Your DMDoS display, with a web app

OpenDMD is free, open-source software for the display. It plays the GIFs already on your SD card, and adds a web app for your phone or computer: skip and pause, build playlists, set the brightness and colour, and dim the panel at night by itself.

Control it from your phone

Open opendmd.local on any phone, tablet or computer on your WiFi. Skip, pause, or step back through what just played, and see what's coming next. Recently played lists the last GIFs on the panel: tap one to see it again.

On a computer, Space pauses and the arrow keys skip and go back. A tour of the web app

Follows RePlayOS

Play games on a RePlayOS box, and the display can follow it. When a game starts, the panel shows the console's logo or name, a title card, then GIFs of that console, with the game's own coming up most. In the menus it plays a playlist you pick.

Nothing is installed on the box: turn on its NET CONTROL, then type its address and code into Settings. When the box is off, the display plays as usual. Set it up

Playlists

Tick GIFs in the Library, or a whole folder, and add them to a playlist; the one playing comes first. Or fill one by platform, category and words in the title.

Reorder, rename and duplicate playlists, mark favourites to play more often, and switch the panel to another in a few seconds. More about playlists

Brightness and colour

Set the brightness from Now or Settings, with a maximum so the panel stays within what its power supply can give.

Show everything in full colour, or in one colour like a classic dot-matrix display: amber, red, green, blue or white. Add a soft phosphor glow, and choose how the panel moves from one GIF to the next. The Panel settings

Night mode and schedules

Night mode dims the panel or turns it off every night: between times you choose, or from sunset to sunrise where the display is, following the seasons by itself. Wake for an hour from Now when you want it back.

Schedule rules pick a playlist or a brightness for certain days and times, and for part of the year if you like. If your card has the Halloween or Christmas playlist, one press adds a rule that plays it in its season. The schedule

What you need

  • A DMDoS displayTwo LED panels, an ESP32 board and a microSD card. dmdos.net has the parts list and a build guide with photos.
  • A computer, onceChrome, Edge, Opera or Firefox 151+ and a USB cable, to install OpenDMD.
  • Your WiFiThe display joins your 2.4 GHz WiFi, and you use it from any browser on the same network.

OpenDMD and DMDoS

DMDoS is a community project from Spain. The display, its board by MORTACA, the build guide and the GIF pack all come from dmdos.net. OpenDMD only replaces the software on the display's ESP32 board, and the original DMDoS software can be put back from the Software section of dmdos.net.

OpenDMD is an independent project, not made by or affiliated with DMDoS or MORTACA. To build it yourself or see how it works, read For developers.

# The guide

How to set up OpenDMD and get the most from your display. Start with What
you need, then Install. <a href="/demo/">The demo</a> runs the web app in
your browser, with no display needed.

## Getting started

<!-- site:features -->

- **[What you need](../../docs/guide/hardware.md):** The display, where its
  parts come from, and what OpenDMD expects of them.
- **[Install](../../docs/guide/install.md):** From your browser in about a
  minute, then onto your WiFi.

## Every day

<!-- site:features -->

- **[The web app](../../docs/guide/web-app.md):** Now, Library, Playlists,
  Formulas and Settings, view by view.
- **[Create Art](../../docs/guide/create-art.md):** Make a GIF for the panel
  from game art, an old GeoCities GIF or your own picture.
- **[Formulas](../../docs/guide/formulas.md):** Play them, change them, write
  your own and share them.
- **[The SD card](../../docs/guide/card.md):** Where GIFs go, and how their
  names become details like the game and the system.

## Keeping it running

<!-- site:features -->

- **[Updating](../../docs/guide/updating.md):** New versions over WiFi, or
  from the installer.
- **[Troubleshooting](../../docs/guide/troubleshooting.md):** When the
  installer, the WiFi or the card won't play along.
